# Persönliches — guidance for the assistant

This section covers the taxpayer(s) themselves, not income or assets.

- **Steuerpflichtige Personen**: legal name, AHVN13, confession (for church tax), and 2nd-pillar (pension fund) membership. Usually just needs the prior year's data confirmed unless something changed (marriage, moving to ZH, confession change).
- **Kinder / Unterstützte Personen**: dependants who affect deductions. Only fill in if the household composition changed since last year.
- **Vertreter**: a third party allowed to act on the taxpayer's behalf (e.g. a fiduciary). Leave blank unless the user has one.
- **Erhaltene / Ausgerichtete Schenkungen und Erbschaften**: gifts or inheritances received or given above the cantonal reporting threshold. Ask the user directly; this is rarely present in uploaded documents.
- **Kapitalleistungen**: lump-sum payments (e.g. pension fund capital withdrawal, insurance payout) — taxed separately from ordinary income, so flag these clearly rather than folding them into "Einkünfte".
- **Bankverbindung für Rückerstattungen**: the IBAN and account holder name the tax office should refund any withholding-tax credit to. Confirmed live fields: "IBAN-Nr." and "Konto lautend auf". Safe to prefill from a bank document or from the user directly if not already on file.

When suggesting values, prefer whatever the user already confirmed for the prior tax period unless a new document contradicts it.
