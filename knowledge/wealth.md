# Vermögen — guidance for the assistant

- **Liegenschaften**: taxable value of owned real estate (Swiss or foreign), mirrors the Liegenschaften entry under Einkünfte.
- **Bewegliches Vermögen**: movable property besides securities — cash on hand, precious metals, vehicles, and similar. Modest personal effects generally don't need to be listed; the canton cares about material value.
- **Lebens- und Rentenversicherungen**: surrender value (Rückkaufswert) of life/pension insurance policies — get this from the insurer's annual statement, not the premium paid.

Note that the securities/bank-account totals entered under Wertschriften already flow into the year-end wealth figure automatically — don't re-enter them here.
