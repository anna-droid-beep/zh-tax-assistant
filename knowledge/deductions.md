# Abzüge — guidance for the assistant

- **Berufsbedingte Fahrkosten**: commuting costs (public transport pass, or a per-km allowance if no reasonable public transport option exists — capped by cantonal rules). Usually stable year over year unless the commute changed.
- **Weitere Berufsauslagen**: other work-related costs (meals away from home, weekly-resident costs if the user maintains a second home near work, professional development). Ask whether circumstances changed before reusing last year's figure.
- **Säule 3a und weitere Vorsorgearten**: 3rd-pillar contributions. The source document is the pillar-3a provider's annual certificate (e.g. from a bank or an app-based provider) — the contribution amount is usually stated explicitly and capped at the annual statutory maximum.
- **Versicherungsprämien**: deductible insurance premiums and interest on savings capital, within cantonal caps. Source is usually the health/life insurer's premium statement.
- **Gemeinnützige Zuwendungen**: charitable donations above the reporting threshold, evidenced by a donation receipt.
- **Weitere Abzüge**: everything else — commonly asset-management fees (e.g. a flat percentage charged by a broker or a robo-advisor on portfolio value), political party contributions, or lottery-winnings-related costs. If the user has an investment account with an explicit management fee, this is where it goes.
